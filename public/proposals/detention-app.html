03 · The money

The invoice is the carrier's. The money goes straight to the driver.

Detention is owed to the carrier on the rate confirmation. So every invoice goes out in the carrier's name, and the money goes into the carrier's own Stripe account. We never own the claim and never hold anyone's money. That keeps the app simple to run, and simple to approve for Stripe and the app stores.

Where the money goes

BROKERPays the invoice by bank transfer to its own account number, or online.
STRIPEMatches the payment to the invoice and tells the app in real time.
YOUYour fee is taken automatically, per paid invoice.
DRIVERThe rest lands in their bank, usually within 2 business days.

Verification without friction

No licence photos. Two checks we need anyway do the job:

  • The MC / DOT number is checked against the federal carrier database at sign-up. One field.
  • Stripe verifies identity and bank before the first payout. Fake accounts can't get paid.
  • Carriers that use a factoring company are asked once; invoices then follow the factor's rules.

How you earn

SubscriptionA monthly or yearly plan, through the App Store and Google Play.
Fee per invoiceA percentage of every detention invoice paid, taken by Stripe automatically.
Or bothThe app supports either model, so you can test what drivers prefer. No pay, no fee sells itself.
