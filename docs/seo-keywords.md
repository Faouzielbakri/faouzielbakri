Data: DataForSEO. Volumes are monthly searches; difficulty 0–100 (under ~20 =
winnable quickly by a page with real expertise + links).

- **Pass 1–3** — DataForSEO Labs (Google, **US, en**), July 2026. ≈ $0.20.
- **Pass 4** — Google Ads search volume + Labs bulk difficulty, August 2026. ≈ $0.19.
  Two locales: **Morocco / fr (2504)** and **US / en (2840)**.

## The funnel

1. **Developers & AI-search citations** (informational) → blog posts
2. **Founders & business owners** (problem-aware, pre-"developer") → blog posts with strong /hire CTAs
3. **Recruiters & clients ready to hire** (commercial) → /hire landing pages

Each tier links down to the next: post → case studies → /hire → contact.

The whole Moroccan developer market across every city is worth roughly
**200 searches/mo at ~$2 CPC** — under $400/mo of traffic value if you captured
100% of it. One click on "hire full stack developer" is worth $123. A dozen
near-identical city pages would also be a textbook doorway-page pattern with no
Google Business Profile behind them.

**Local demand does exist, but for a different service:** "creation site web
maroc" (1,000/mo, $1.85), "agence web maroc" (390/mo, $1.19), "création site web
casablanca" (210/mo, $1.08). That's cheap website-agency work in French, against
the site's whole positioning. Deliberately not pursued — revisit only if the
business goal changes to local agency work, and then it's a separate French site,
not pages bolted onto this one.

## Operating notes

- One new tier-2 post ≈ one focused writing session; always end with case-study
  links + /hire CTA.
- Re-check rankings in GSC ~4 weeks after each publish; iterate titles and
  descriptions on CTR, not position.
- Landing pages carry `Service` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD and are
  registered in `sitemap.ts` **and** `llms.txt/route.ts` — both, every time.
- DataForSEO (balance ≈ $33.5 after pass 4). Useful endpoints and real costs:
  `keywords_data/google_ads/search_volume/live` = **$0.09 per request** for up to
  1,000 keywords (best value by far — batch aggressively);
  `dataforseo_labs/google/bulk_keyword_difficulty/live` ≈ **$0.014**;
  `keyword_suggestions/live` ≈ $0.013 per seed; SERP live regular ≈ $0.002.
- Always pass an explicit `location_code` / `language_code`. The July pass's
  Morocco rejection was right by luck, not by method.
