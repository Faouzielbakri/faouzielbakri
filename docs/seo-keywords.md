# SEO keyword strategy — research log & content roadmap

Data: DataForSEO Labs (Google, US, en), July 2026. Three research passes,
total API spend ≈ $0.20. Volumes are monthly searches; difficulty 0–100
(under ~20 = winnable quickly by a page with real expertise + links).

## The funnel

1. **Developers & AI-search citations** (informational) → blog posts
2. **Founders & business owners** (problem-aware, pre-"developer") → blog posts with strong /hire CTAs
3. **Recruiters & clients ready to hire** (commercial) → /hire landing page

Each tier links down to the next: post → case studies → /hire → contact.

## Rejected

- French/Morocco hiring terms ("developpeur web maroc" etc.): ~10 searches/mo — not worth pages.
- "rag llm" head terms (2.9K/49): definitional queries owned by AWS/IBM/Databricks glossaries; citations come from the architecture post instead.
- "whatsapp bot for business": no measurable volume in suggestions.

## Operating notes

- One new tier-2 post ≈ one focused writing session; always end with case-study links + /hire CTA.
- Re-check rankings in GSC ~4 weeks after each publish; iterate titles/descriptions on CTR, not position.
- DataForSEO account (balance ~$38.7): keyword_suggestions/live ≈ $0.013 per seed; SERP live regular ≈ $0.002.
